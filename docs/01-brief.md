# Salon Queue Brief

## Problem
During busy hours and weekends, salon customers may wait for long periods without knowing when their turn will come. This creates confusion about queue order and makes it difficult for receptionists to coordinate customers across multiple barbers.

## Users
Customers, receptionist/owner, and barbers.

## Must-have features
1. Customers can join the queue using their phones, select one or more services, and choose a preferred barber or "Any Barber".
2. Customers can view their live queue position, estimated waiting time, and receive a "You're next!" alert.
3. Receptionists can add walk-in customers, call the next customer, and mark customers as no-shows.
4. Barbers can view their current customer and use Start/Done actions to manage services.
5. A waiting-area TV display shows the customer currently being served by each barber and the next five tokens.
6. Owners can manage services, including their duration and price, manage barbers on duty, and open or close the queue.

## Don't build
Online payments, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp messaging, loyalty programmes, or multiple branches.

## Look & feel
A premium salon design with a dark charcoal background, gold accents, clean mobile-first layouts, and English and Gujarati labels.
